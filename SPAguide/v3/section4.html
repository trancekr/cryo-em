3D reconstruction
2D projections correspond to slices through 3D Fourier space
The Fourier transform of each 2D class average represents a central section through the Fourier transform of the initial object.
Different viewing directions fill the 3D Fourier volume, so orientation assignment determines where each slice is placed.
The inverse FT of the combined sections generates the initial object.
cryoEM101 Chapter 5 · De Rosier & Klug, Nature (1968)

Resolution
X-ray crystallography and cryo-EM use different resolution criteria

X-ray crystallography

Resolution is calculated from diffraction geometry using Bragg's law. Higher-angle diffraction corresponds to smaller resolvable spacings.

Cryo-EM

Resolution is estimated from Fourier Shell Correlation between independently refined half-maps. The 0.143 threshold is commonly used.

Rosenthal & Henderson, J Mol Biol (2003)

Problems & limitations
Sample, microscopy, and processing still limit cryo-EM
Sample
  1. Size limitation
  2. Requires highly purified sample
  3. Vitrification process
Microscopy
  1. Beam damage from the electron beam
  2. Low dose imaging
  3. Noisy images with low signal-to-noise ratio
  4. Imperfect imaging artifacts
Data processing
  1. Complex image processing: particle picking, alignment, reconstruction
  2. Heterogeneous, dynamic, and continuous conformations
  3. Multiple possible interpretations of the data
  4. Computing power!!!
