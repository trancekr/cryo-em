Total electron exposure
Balance particle signal against dose-dependent radiation damage
Typical total exposure
40–60 e−/Å²
Limited total exposure is used because high-resolution signal decays with dose.
Low-dose limit
High-resolution signal is lost first during exposure.
Sensitive side-chain density can fade at ~3 e−/Å².
Optimal exposure
40–60 e−/Å² is the practical range for SPA movies.
Set this total exposure before dividing it into frames.
High-dose limit
Radiation damage sets the upper limit for total exposure.
Avoid excess dose above 60 e−/Å².
Dose decision
Choose a total exposure range before optimizing other acquisition settings.

Hole targeting strategy
Trade throughput against beam-induced movement and aberrations
Conventional targeting
One shot per hole, single recentering
  • 1 shot per hole · 4 stage moves
  • Stable, but slow throughput
Beam-image shift
Multi-hole targeting from one stage
  • 4 shots per stage move
  • Aberrations rise with shift distance
Multi-shot per hole
Several exposures inside one hole
  • 4 shots per hole · minimal stage motion
  • Charging & ice damage compound
